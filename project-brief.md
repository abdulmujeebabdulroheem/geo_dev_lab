#My project brief

## The Question

Which offshore areas south of Lagos, Nigeria, have relatively gentle seabed slopes for preliminary subsea pipeline corridor screening?


## Why it matters

Seabed slope drives a lot of subsea pipeline routing cost and risk. Steeper slopes mean higher installation cost, more geotechnical uncertainty, and greater risk of slope instability along the route. A preliminary slope screening lets a routing engineer or an offshore developer narrow down which corridors are even worth commissioning expensive site-specific geophysical or geotechnical surveys on, before that money gets spent.
